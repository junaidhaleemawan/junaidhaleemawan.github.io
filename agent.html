One agent, four jobs done at once.

      
A manual intake desk handles first replies, qualification questions, data entry into the CRM and callback scheduling. This agent does all four at the same time, for every lead.

      

        
1. Reply instantly
Answers every inbound enquiry from your website, WhatsApp or a Meta ad within seconds, at any time of day.

        
2. Ask the right questions
Collects budget, timeline and location in a natural conversation instead of a form.

        
3. Score the lead
Ranks each lead as hot, warm or cold as the answers come in, so reps know who to call first.

        
4. Book or nurture
Hot and warm leads get a callback slot. Cold leads go into a follow-up sequence. Everything is logged, with no manual data entry.
